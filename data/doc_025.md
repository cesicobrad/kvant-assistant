# Notes on Voyage embeddings for Slovenian — preliminary findings

*Rok Lampret — 2025-01-22*

Quick notes from a few hours of testing yesterday. Not a proper eval, don't cite this anywhere official.

---

**Background**

We've been using text-embedding-3-large as the default for basically everything. Voyage AI (voyage-2 and voyage-large-2) has been on my list to test since someone mentioned it in the LlamaIndex Discord. The specific question: does it handle Slovenian-language queries better, and is the cost difference worth it.

**What I tested**

Small test set, 40 queries total, pulled from the NLB project — mix of factual lookups, vague intent queries, and a few that are basically untranslatable bureaucratic Slovenian ("uveljavitev pravice do predčasnega dviga" etc.). Indexed about 800 short document chunks. Compared retrieval quality by hand, top-5 results per query.

**Findings so far**

Honestly mixed. voyage-large-2 does seem to handle some of the morphologically complex Slovenian queries better — there were 4-5 cases where it retrieved the right chunk and text-embedding-3-large didn't, specifically on queries with declined nouns where the document used a different case form. That's real and it matters.

But text-embedding-3-large still won on the majority of queries, and by a comfortable margin on English-heavy or mixed-language content (NLB has a lot of that — regulatory docs are half English). voyage-2 (the cheaper one) was noticeably worse than both on Slovenian, don't bother.

**Cost**

voyage-large-2 is roughly 12x more expensive per token than text-embedding-3-small and about 3x more than text-embedding-3-large at current pricing. For a corpus the size of NLB's this would add up. Hard to justify right now without a more rigorous eval showing consistent wins.

**What's unclear**

Whether the Slovenian improvements are voyage-specific or just a function of model size. Didn't test BGE multilingual, which I should. Also only tested one domain — legal/financial — might behave differently on something like Pipistrel's technical manuals.

---

Needs more testing before we recommend this to anyone. Žiga to follow up Q1 with a proper RAGAS run on a larger query set.
